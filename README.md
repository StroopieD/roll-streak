# Roll Streak

BJJ and workout tracker with streaks. No login. Sessions are saved on the device you use, and you can export them to Notion.

## Use it
Open https://stroopied.github.io/roll-streak/ on your phone and choose Share > Add to Home Screen. Opening it from the Home Screen works best for keeping your data.

## Backups and Notion
- **Export backup** (Stats tab) saves all sessions as a file. Use **Import backup** to restore them, or to move them to another device.
- **Export mat sessions for Notion (CSV)** creates a file with the BJJ sessions. In your BJJ Daily Class Tracker in Notion, use Import > CSV (or Merge with CSV) to add them.

## Things to know
- Each device keeps its own sessions. Laptop and phone won't match automatically.
- Phone browsers can clear website data for sites you haven't used for a while. Export a backup now and then.
- Deleting a session in the app does not delete its Notion row.
